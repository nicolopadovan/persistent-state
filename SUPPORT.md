# Support

- Read the [documentation](https://persistent-state.nicolopadovan.com).
- Ask a question in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/nicolopadovan/persistent-state/discussions/categories/q-a).
- Search the [issues](https://github.com/nicolopadovan/persistent-state/issues), then open a new one for a bug or a
  feature request.

When you report a bug, include:

- the `@nicolopadovan/persistent-state` version, your framework and its version;
- the definition options you use, without secrets;
- the storage and consent state you expected and what you observed;
- the steps to reproduce.

For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.
